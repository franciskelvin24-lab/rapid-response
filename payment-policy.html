CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Payment and refund policy.

Last updated: 5 October 2026. This editable policy is a starting point; the business owner should review it for each service and course before publication.

Payment methods

Where an item has a published fee and checkout is enabled, payment is processed securely through Flutterwave. Available methods may include card, bank transfer and USSD. Never send card details to RRSG by email or WhatsApp.

Payment confirmation

A browser payment message is not confirmation that a service is booked or a learner is enrolled. RRSG will verify each transaction in the Flutterwave dashboard or through server-side verification, then contact the customer to confirm next steps.

Quotes, deposits and services

Items marked “Contact us for fees” require a quote. A payment link or deposit option applies only when explicitly provided for that item. Payment alone does not confirm officer, vehicle, date or service availability; RRSG must confirm those arrangements separately.

Courses

Course registration and payment do not automatically provide course access or a certificate. RRSG will confirm the payment and course details with the registrant. Any course-specific cancellation or transfer terms should be supplied by RRSG before payment.

Refunds and cancellation

Contact RRSG promptly at info@rapidresponseprotocol.com or +234 706 4440 021 with your name, item and transaction reference to request help with a duplicate, mistaken or disputed payment. Refund eligibility, timing, cancellation charges and any non-refundable deposits must be agreed with RRSG and confirmed in writing for your particular booking or course. Flutterwave processing times and applicable bank terms may also affect how quickly a refund appears.

Questions

Contact Rapid Response Protocol and Logistics Limited before paying if any fee, deposit, schedule or refund condition is unclear.
